Under the hood

How the genetic algorithm works

Four ideas turn random wiggling into a deliberate gait.

Selection pressure

Each generation, parents are chosen by tournament selection: pick five creatures at random, keep the fittest. The best three genomes also survive untouched (elitism), so progress is never lost.

Crossover

Two parents are recombined with uniform crossover — each gene is inherited from one parent or the other. Good "half-solutions" from different lineages get a chance to combine.

Mutation that anneals

Genes receive small Gaussian nudges. Mutation strength starts large to explore, then shrinks each generation to fine-tune — so improvements are gradual, not random jumps.

A meaningful fitness

Fitness rewards rightward distance only, minus an energy cost, and is frozen the instant a creature flips airborne. Spinning, drifting, and hopping exploits all score zero — walking wins.

Fitness fitness = max(0, forward_distance − 0.015 · energy − launch_penalty)
